METHOD / 01

THE RULES

Cases are included when a person held one of the named offices and was arrested or formally criminally charged with a sex-related offense between 1976 and 2026. The prominence test keeps the list focused on titles a child would recognize and mayors with substantial regional or national visibility. One labeled exception: Donald J. Trump is included on a civil court’s sexual-abuse liability finding and the judge’s published characterization of it as rape — his card states plainly that there was no arrest, criminal charge, or criminal conviction.

METHOD / 02

THE LANGUAGE

“Pedophile” and “sex offender” are not editorial shortcuts here. The cards say what the record supports: alleged, charged, indicted, pleaded guilty, convicted, dismissed, or affirmed. A dropped charge is not a conviction.

We are not using the word “gay” in a pejorative manner. The reason we list “gay” in the list of crimes and perversions is due to hypocrisy. If a politician is gay and public about it — and they end up on this list — they will not get the GAY tag, but every other tag is possible. If they are presenting themselves as straight, and they end up on this list — they get the GAY tag.

METHOD / 03

THE LIMIT

This is research, not a live court docket. Open-web archives can be incomplete, paywalled, sealed, inconsistently indexed, or overtaken by later developments. Read the linked primary records and reporting before drawing conclusions.
